MapsToTom ships the TTconv files unchanged, except fixdate/rc.gltt and fixdate/ttconv.sh:
- rc.gltt restarts the converter whenever it quits, logs each restart to gl/log/ttconv.log,
  and can log the GPS engine to gl/log/gltt.txt when the file gl/debug-gltt exists;
- ttconv.sh (the TTconv menu button) restarts the GPS driver and the converter cleanly.
